# PiNova Global Marketplace — User Guide

> **Permanent Transparency & Operational Notices:**
> - **Transparency Notice:** PiNova Global Marketplace is built on a non-custodial marketplace architecture. Payment processing relies on the Official Pi SDK v2 and Pi Platform API. PiNova never stores or manages Pi wallet private keys, recovery phrases, passphrases, blockchain infrastructure, or official Pi Network services.
> - **Operational Notice:** Certain marketplace capabilities rely on external service providers and official Pi Platform services. Feature availability, response times, and service outcomes may vary depending on provider availability, network connectivity, and Official Pi Platform service status.

## Welcome to PiNova!

### 1. Browsing & Shopping
- Explore physical goods, digital assets, mobile top-ups, and utility services across all implemented modules.
- Filter products by category, price, country, or vendor performance metrics and verified seller ratings.
- Use the **AI Concierge** (Gemini 3.6 Flash powered) to ask natural language questions like *"Find solar chargers under 50 Pi"*. Note: Gemini AI provides recommendations and operational assistance only; verified marketplace information is displayed separately from AI-generated content.

### 2. Paying with Pi (Protected Checkout Experience)
- Click **Pay with Pi** on checkout or utility bills to enter the **Protected Checkout Experience**.
- Authorize payment inside your official Pi Browser wallet using official Pi SDK v2 workflows. Payment approval and completion are processed through the Official Pi SDK v2 payment workflow and Pi Platform API according to their documented integration flow. PiNova never performs wallet custody, settlement, blockchain validation, or transaction finality.
- Receive digital receipts and order tracking updates under Marketplace Order Protection.

### 3. Utility Bills & Mobile Airtime
- Select your provider (e.g. Safaricom, MTN, IKEDC, DSTV).
- Enter account or phone number.
- Perform **application-level service availability checks** to check account status before submitting Pi payment.

### 4. Marketplace Dispute Management Workflow
- Submit return requests or order issues to trigger the AI-assisted dispute management workflow for fast resolution.

### 5. Changing Languages
- Click the language selector in the top bar.
- Choose from 23 global languages (including English, Hausa, Arabic, French, Spanish, Chinese, Vietnamese, etc.).
