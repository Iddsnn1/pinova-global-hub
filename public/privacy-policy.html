Privacy Policy

PiNova Global Hub · Effective date: 15 November 2026 · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In brief: We use information to operate the marketplace and related services, authenticate users, protect accounts and orders, support vendors, and respond to requests. We do not ask for or store Pi wallet passphrases or private keys, and we do not sell personal information.

1. Who we are

PiNova Global Hub (“PiNova”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a digital marketplace and services platform operated by DANDATTI GLOBAL VENTURES LIMITED. For privacy questions, contact support@iddsnn.com.

2. Information we may process

  • Pi account and authentication information: information made available through the Pi Platform authorization flow, such as a Pi username and account identifier, plus authentication credentials needed to validate a session. We use these only for sign-in, account security and platform functions.
  • Orders and marketplace activity: cart, order, product, seller, delivery, dispute and transaction-status details needed to provide marketplace services and maintain records.
  • Information you provide: contact details, support messages, profile details, and shipping or service information you choose to submit.
  • Vendor verification documents: where vendor verification is requested, documents and details you submit for identity or business review. These are restricted and stored using encryption controls. Do not submit documents unless the relevant verification flow asks for them.
  • Technical and security information: basic request, device/browser, diagnostic and security-event information generated when the service is used.

Do not send us Pi wallet passphrases, private keys, recovery phrases, or passwords. PiNova does not need them and will never request them.

3. How we use information

We process information to provide and maintain the service; authenticate users and validate Pi Platform sessions; manage listings, orders, delivery and support; conduct vendor verification and fraud prevention; protect the platform; troubleshoot errors; meet legal obligations; and communicate important service or policy updates.

4. Pi Network and other service providers

When you choose to connect a Pi account, the Pi Platform handles its own authorization and may share the information described in its consent screen. PiNova receives only the information made available through the authorized integration and uses it for the purposes described here. Your use of Pi Network services is also subject to Pi Network's own terms and privacy information.

We use hosting, database, storage, security and other technology providers to operate PiNova. Information may be processed by those providers on our behalf, under their applicable security and privacy terms. Marketplace vendors, delivery providers or other service partners may receive the information necessary to fulfil a transaction or service you request.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose relevant information to a vendor or service provider to fulfil your request; to technology providers supporting the platform; where required by law or valid legal process; to protect users, rights and platform security; or in connection with a business restructuring subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not share information for unrelated third-party marketing without an appropriate legal basis or your consent.

6. Storage, security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including access controls and encryption for sensitive vendor documents. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes above, including order records, security, dispute handling and legal obligations, and then delete or de-identify it where practicable.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of certain personal information, or object to certain processing. You may also withdraw an optional authorization where the relevant service permits it; withdrawal may prevent use of features that require that information. Contact support@iddsnn.com to make a request. We may need to verify your identity and retain some records where the law requires or permits it.

8. Children

PiNova is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. International processing

Our technology providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we take steps intended to provide appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform or applicable requirements change. The latest version and effective date will be published on this page. Material changes will be communicated through the platform where appropriate.

11. Contact

Privacy enquiries and requests: support@iddsnn.com.
