Platform Order Protection

PiNova’s Platform Order Protection Standard (PSTP) is intended to provide structured order records, status tracking, evidence submission and a process for reviewing disputes between marketplace participants.

Important: PSTP is a platform workflow. It is not, by itself, a regulated escrow service, bank account, insurance policy, payment guarantee or promise that funds can always be recovered. Do not send funds outside the official checkout or follow requests for wallet passphrases.

1. Before you pay

  • Check the seller/provider identity and listing details.
  • Review the total price, currency, delivery method, delivery estimate and seller return/refund policy.
  • Use only the payment method shown by the official PiNova checkout when it is enabled.
  • Do not rely on screenshots or messages as proof of a completed Pi payment.

2. Payment confirmation

Where Pi checkout is enabled, the platform must verify the payment with the Pi Platform server before treating the order as paid. If verification is pending or unavailable, the order may remain pending/processing. Do not repeat a payment solely because a page timed out; first check the order status or contact support.

3. Fulfilment and evidence

Sellers remain responsible for fulfilling the goods or services they offer. Buyers and sellers should retain relevant order references, delivery confirmation, provider receipts and communications. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information in a dispute.

4. Dispute review

Users may submit a dispute through an available in-app workflow or contact idrisdattishanono@gmail.com with the order reference and a concise description. The platform may request relevant evidence from the parties, review the order and payment status, and communicate an outcome under the applicable seller terms and law. Response targets and escalation procedures must be finalized before public launch.

5. Refunds and reversals

Refund eligibility depends on the item/service, seller policy, provider rules, applicable law and the actual payment/settlement state. A refund is not guaranteed merely because a dispute is opened. No user should be asked to provide a wallet passphrase or private key to receive a refund.

6. Limitations and contact

Third-party providers and the Pi Network may experience delays or outages. PSTP cannot guarantee delivery, provider performance or recovery of a transaction. Contact idrisdattishanono@gmail.com for support. Final operator identity, jurisdiction, dispute deadlines and service-level targets must be completed before this notice is approved for public use.
