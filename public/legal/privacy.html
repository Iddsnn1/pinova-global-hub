Privacy Notice

PiNova Global Hub (“PiNova”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a digital hub intended to connect people with marketplace listings, merchant tools, digital services, education-related information and utility discovery. This notice explains the categories of information that may be processed when those features are used.

Pre-launch disclosure: This document is a working privacy notice for Preview review. Before public launch, the operator’s full legal name, business address, applicable governing jurisdiction, final retention periods and the production data inventory must be confirmed. Do not treat this draft as legal advice or as confirmation that every feature is live.

1. Who is responsible

PiNova Global Hub is currently presented under the PiNova Global Hub project name. Public privacy contact: idrisdattishanono@gmail.com. The full legal identity and address of the data controller/operator must be added before public launch.

2. Information that may be processed

  • Pi account and authentication data: Pi username, Pi-provided user identifier, authentication/session information and the permissions granted through the official Pi SDK.
  • Wallet and payment references: wallet address where the user explicitly grants the relevant Pi SDK scope; payment identifiers, transaction identifiers, amounts, status and order references needed to verify a payment and provide support. PiNova does not ask users for wallet passphrases or private keys.
  • Account and merchant information: profile details, store name, contact details, business information and onboarding answers submitted by a Pioneer or merchant.
  • Verification documents: files a merchant chooses or is required to submit for identity or business verification. These are sensitive documents and should be restricted to authorized review processes.
  • Marketplace and service data: products, listings, orders, delivery and fulfilment details, support requests, disputes, invoices, receipts and service-provider responses.
  • Technical and security data: device/browser details, request logs, timestamps, diagnostic events, IP-related security signals and fraud-prevention records.

3. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate users, operate accounts and merchant tools, display listings, create and manage orders, verify Pi payments through the Pi Platform, support fulfilment and disputes, protect users and the service, meet legal obligations, and diagnose reliability or security problems. Optional features may require additional information and should explain that requirement at the point of collection.

4. Pi Network and third-party services

PiNova integrates with the official Pi SDK and Pi Platform APIs. When a user chooses Pi authentication or a Pi payment, relevant information is exchanged with Pi Network to perform the requested operation. Marketplace delivery, currency conversion, communications, hosting, database, storage and other services may also involve independent service providers. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Feature availability depends on provider and network availability.

5. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be shared with the relevant buyer, seller or service provider to complete a transaction; with Pi Network to authenticate or verify a Pi transaction; with contracted infrastructure or security providers; or where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users and the platform. Merchant verification documents must not be exposed through public storefront, catalogue or ordinary status responses.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the stated purpose, transaction records, security, dispute handling and applicable legal requirements. Exact retention periods and deletion procedures are being finalized and must be published before launch. PiNova uses access controls and server-side verification as part of its security design, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Users should never share wallet passphrases or private keys.

7. International use

PiNova is designed for users in multiple countries. Depending on hosting and service-provider locations, information may be processed in countries other than the user’s country. Applicable transfer safeguards and country-specific privacy rights must be confirmed for the final operating entity and deployment.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and to withdraw permissions where processing relies on consent. A request can be sent to idrisdattishanono@gmail.com. We may need to verify the requester’s identity before acting. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security or transaction reasons.

9. Children

PiNova is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information improperly, contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as features, providers and legal requirements change. Material changes should be dated and communicated through the service. Privacy questions: idrisdattishanono@gmail.com.
